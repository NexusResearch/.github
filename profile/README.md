Nexus Research (Or just Nexus) is a community driven non-profit organization focusing on Cybersecurity (Both Offensive and Defensive), Modding/Homebrew (including but not limited to Consoles, Games and Software) and Software/Web Development, with a strong emphasis on privacy. 

Our focus is on Free and Open Source software, whether you are a student, educator, university or an enterprise company. We support the user's freedom (ahoy, did you notice we said users and not consumers?) and we are not limiting our software or forcing age verification on anyone
